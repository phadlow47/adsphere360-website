Our story

We kept seeing the same problems.

Long resale chains. Opaque supply paths. Fraud cleaned up after the money had already moved. Everyone agreed it was a problem, and the supply kept flowing anyway.

So in 2024 we built an exchange that is direct, scanned and transparent by default. We're headquartered in the United States, with operations in Portugal, Spain, Mexico and the Netherlands, close to the publishers and buyers we work with.
