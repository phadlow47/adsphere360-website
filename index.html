Why AdSphere360

Three things we never compromise on.

Quality in programmatic is easy to promise and hard to prove. We built the exchange so every one of these can be checked from the bid request itself.

IVT · HUMAN

Scanned before the auction

All supply is scanned by HUMAN (formerly White Ops) before a bidder sees it. Invalid traffic is filtered out up front, so nobody chases refunds later.

schain · sellers.json

A supply path you can audit

Every bid request carries a complete schain object, and our sellers.json is public and kept current. Buyers see exactly who sits between them and the screen.

Direct only

Publishers we know by name

We contract with publishers directly and never resell other exchanges' inventory. Shorter paths mean more of each dollar reaches the people who make the content.
